About

Nine years of
making things
squish.

I started in a browser-based level editor at fourteen and never really left. These days I split my time between shipping my own atmospheric puzzle games and consulting on "feel" for other studios — the screen shake, the hit-stop, the one extra frame that makes a punch land.

I care less about polygon counts and more about the half-second after you press a button. If a jump doesn't feel good on a grey box with no art, it never will — so that's where I start every project.
